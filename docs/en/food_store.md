# Food & Store
{: .no_toc }

1. TOC
{:toc}

Food increases **hunger** (and sometimes **happiness**). It is bought with **silver**,
which you earn by playing [minigames](minigames.html).

![Food store]({{ '/assets/images/screenshots/02_store.png' | relative_url }})
*The food store with the five foods and their price in silver.*

## Food catalog

| Food | Price (silver) | + Hunger | + Happiness |
|------|:--------------:|:--------:|:-----------:|
| 💧 Water | 5 | +5% | — |
| 🍎 Apple | 10 | +10% | +5% |
| 🍰 Cake | 40 | +15% | +25% |
| 🍔 Burger | 50 | +40% | +10% |
| 🍣 Sushi | 80 | +20% | +30% |

> The burger is the most efficient for **hunger**; sushi and cake give the most
> **happiness**. Values are added and capped at 100%.
{: .tip }

## Buying and feeding

1. Open the **food store** from the main screen.
2. Buy the desired food (silver is deducted).
3. Food is stored in the **refrigerator** (inventory).
4. Open the refrigerator and select a food item to give it to Bob.

Feeding also counts toward the daily mission **"Yummy Time"** (see
[Daily Missions](daily_missions.html)).

## Refrigerator (inventory)

The refrigerator keeps track of how much of each food you have bought. Each time
you feed Bob, one unit is deducted. If a food item reaches 0, it disappears
from the inventory.
